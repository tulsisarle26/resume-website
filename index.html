About me

I’m a student pursuing Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. I’m passionate about learning, creativity, business, and exploring new opportunities.

Content writing

Content writing is something I genuinely enjoy. I like turning ideas into words that are clear, interesting and easy to read, whether it is a short caption, a blog post or a longer article. For me, good writing starts with understanding the reader: who they are, what they want to know, and how to keep them interested until the last line.

Studying Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore has shown me how powerful words are in marketing and brand building, because the right message can build trust and bring people to a business. My digital marketing learning, including my HubSpot certification, has also taught me why simple language, a clear structure and search-friendly content matter. I keep improving by reading widely, practising regularly and paying attention to the small details that make a piece of writing feel polished. I am excited to keep growing as a writer and to use this skill in my future work as an entrepreneur.
